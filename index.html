A proposed workflow, not a live service

Keep the control
with the business.

The concept is draft-only. A person confirms facts, decides how to handle exceptions, and uses the business's own account if they choose to publish.

01 / INPUT
Start with authorized facts.

Review text and confirmed business details. Do not paste customer or private data into this sample.

02 / REVIEW
Separate a draft from a decision.

Serious complaints, spam and conflicting inputs should not turn into unreviewed public statements.

03 / APPROVE
The client decides whether to publish.

No response on this page is submitted to a review platform.
